Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

In short: no accounts, no cookies, no analytics. Showing the owners’ Discord profiles makes your browser contact Discord and similar services, which can see your IP address like any website you visit.

1. Overview

This policy explains what information is handled when you use Ruby Hub (“we,” “us”). Ruby Hub is a static website. We do not require accounts, do not run our own analytics or advertising trackers, and do not set cookies.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through the website itself. If you contact us on Discord, we receive what you send us, such as your Discord username, user ID, and message content.

3. Hosting Providers

The site is hosted by a third party (such as GitHub Pages). Hosts may automatically log technical data like IP address, browser type, device, pages requested, and timestamps for security and operations. We do not control these logs. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

4. Third-Party Requests

To display the owners’ Discord profiles, your browser loads images and data from third parties, such as Discord’s CDN (cdn.discordapp.com), Lanyard (api.lanyard.rest), and a public Discord lookup API. It also loads fonts from Google Fonts. These services receive your IP address and standard browser details when your browser contacts them, under their own privacy policies. We do not receive that data. If you prefer not to make these requests, do not use this site.

5. Local Features

The search box, filters, and “Copy script” button run in your browser. Searches and copied content are not sent to us. We do not store anything in your browser.

6. Scripts You Run

Scripts run inside other platforms, not on this website. How they handle data depends on what they do, and you should review any code before running it. We may embed non-personal identifiers or watermarks in scripts to detect unauthorized copying or leaks. These are not designed to collect personal information.

7. How We Use Information

We use messages you send us only to respond, to provide support, to handle takedown, legal, abuse, or leak-related matters, and to enforce our Terms. We do not sell or rent personal information and do not use it for advertising.

8. Sharing and Disclosure

We may disclose information if required by law or valid legal process, to enforce our Terms (including reporting leaks or abuse to platforms and hosting providers), or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Ruby Hub, our users, or others.

9. Retention and Security

We keep messages only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. We use reasonable measures to protect the site, but no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of personal information we hold about you. Because we hold very little, requests usually concern messages you sent us. Contact us on Discord and we will respond within a reasonable time. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

12. International Users

Hosts and third parties above may process data in countries other than your own. By using the Service you understand your data may be transferred and processed there.

13. Changes

If we change how we handle information, we will update this policy and its date before the change takes effect. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.
