Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ruby Hub (“we,” “us”) handles information when you use our website. We keep this simple because we collect very little.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not run our own analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies. This site is a static website and does not store personal information about you on our own servers.

2. Hosting and Server Logs

Ruby Hub is hosted on a third-party service (such as GitHub Pages). Hosting providers may automatically collect technical data like your IP address, browser type, pages requested, and timestamps for security and operations. We do not control this data; see your host’s privacy policy (for GitHub, GitHub’s Privacy Statement).

3. What Stays in Your Browser

The “Copy Script” button uses your browser’s clipboard feature only when you click it. Nothing you copy or search for is sent to us.

4. Third-Party Services and Links

If you click links to third-party sites or services (for example GitHub or Discord), or if scripts you run fetch code from third-party hosts, those parties may collect information under their own policies. We are not responsible for their practices. Scripts you run in other platforms are separate from this website and may behave differently; review them before use.

5. If You Contact Us

If you message us on Discord, we receive the information you send (such as your Discord username and message). We use it only to respond, handle takedown or legal notices, and keep reasonable records, and we do not sell it.

6. How We Use and Share Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may disclose information if required by law, to enforce our Terms, or to protect rights and safety.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of personal information we hold. Because we hold almost none, these requests usually concern messages you have sent us. Message us on Discord and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the site, but no method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

10. Changes

If we add features that collect data (such as analytics), we will update this policy and its date before doing so. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: contact us on Discord render.xyz.
